Stytch is a hosted authentication and identity service for apps. An app calls its API and SDKs for sign-in and organization management, and Stytch runs the service on its own servers. It supports single sign-on over SAML and OIDC, and it defines custom roles and permissions for users.

Its own comparison pages position it as an alternative to Auth0 and Amazon Cognito, and its [migration guide](https://stytch.com/docs/get-started/guides/migration-and-deployment) covers moving existing users over from Auth0, Cognito and Firebase. Stytch is part of Twilio.

## Worth knowing

Stytch is a hosted service only. No self-hosted install was found, so the record covers the hosted product alone.
