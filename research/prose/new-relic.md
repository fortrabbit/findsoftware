New Relic is a hosted observability platform. It collects telemetry from applications, infrastructure, logs and browsers, and it accepts OpenTelemetry data from the OpenTelemetry SDKs and the OpenTelemetry Collector. The [pricing page](https://newrelic.com/pricing) lists the editions and user types.

## Worth knowing

The platform is offered only as a hosted service. Data can be stored in an EU data center.
