TimelineJS is a JavaScript library that presents dated slides, with text and media, as a timeline in a web page. A page loads the stylesheet and the script, then creates a timeline in an element; the slide data comes from a JSON object or from a Google Spreadsheet. The docs also describe an authoring tool for people who do not write code, which produces the markup for the embed.

## Worth knowing

The authoring tool and the hosting on Knight Lab's systems are a hosted service next to the open-source library. A timeline that reads its slides from a Google Spreadsheet depends on that spreadsheet staying in place.
