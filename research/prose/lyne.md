Lyne Components are the web components of the Lyne Design System, which SBB uses for its websites and for customer and employee applications. The components are built with Lit and published on npm as @sbb-esta/lyne-elements. The getting-started guide covers plain JavaScript, Angular and React/Next.js.

The components are maintained by SBB AG.

## Worth knowing

The README says the software is unsupported unless a support statement says otherwise, and is used at the user's own risk.
