Tally is a hosted online form builder in which forms are written as text documents. The forms run on the vendor's servers, and a site receives one as an embed, a popup or a link. The vendor's API and webhooks send form submissions to other software.

The homepage says the forms and submissions are unlimited on the free tier, within the vendor's fair use guidelines.

## Worth knowing

The vendor is Tally BV, a company in Belgium, and form data is held in Europe. Team access to workspaces is on the Pro plan, according to the pricing page.
