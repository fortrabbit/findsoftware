Athos Commerce, formerly Klevu, is a hosted search service for online stores. Its [site search page](https://athoscommerce.com/products/site-search/) describes an AI-powered site search solution, and the [documentation](https://docs.klevu.com/) covers APIs for indexing data and for integrating the search into a storefront.

The product is sold as a service on the company's cloud infrastructure. No self-hosted version is named on the company's pages, so the search server is not something a shop runs itself.

## Worth knowing

The Klevu name now sits under Athos Commerce, which also covers the Searchspring and Intelligent Reach brands. Plans are listed on the [pricing page](https://athoscommerce.com/pricing/).
