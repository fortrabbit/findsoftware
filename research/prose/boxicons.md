Boxicons is an icon library whose free icons come as SVG files and font files. The npm packages are published under the @boxicons scope for JavaScript, React, Vue and Svelte, and the [core package](https://docs.boxicons.com/core) holds the icon SVGs and font files the others build on. A command-line tool installs the packages.

The [introduction](https://docs.boxicons.com/introduction) says Boxicons Pro unlocks the full library and all tools. The Pro tier is sold under its own license agreement, separate from the licenses that cover the free icons.

## Worth knowing

The Pro tier is a paid plan, and its license covers individual and team use.
