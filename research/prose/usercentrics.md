Usercentrics is a hosted consent management platform. A consent banner on a website records what the visitor chose and holds back tracking elements until consent is given. The company is Usercentrics GmbH, based in Munich, and its pricing page states that hosting is in a data center in Germany.

The product is sold as Web CMP and App CMP. Banner text can be shown in the visitor's browser language. The pricing page lists a CMP configuration API, including a GraphQL API for complex configurations, and describes Google Consent Mode support.

## Worth knowing

The terms name a second contracting entity in the United States, Usercentrics Inc. The number of banner languages depends on the plan. The software is a hosted service, and the record holds no self-hosted option.
