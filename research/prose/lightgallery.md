lightGallery is a JavaScript and TypeScript lightbox and gallery for images and video. It has no dependencies. Native packages wrap the same core for React, Vue and Angular, and the core is also published on its own as @lightgallery/headless.

## Worth knowing

Projects distributed under GPLv3-compatible terms use lightGallery under the GPL. Projects that keep their source proprietary need the commercial license, and commercial projects set a license key in the gallery's settings.
