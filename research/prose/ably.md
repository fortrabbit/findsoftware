Ably is a hosted realtime messaging service, maintained by Ably Realtime Ltd. of London. Applications connect to it through its SDKs and APIs, and the company's about page describes it as a pub/sub platform for WebSocket connections.

Ably runs as a service on the company's infrastructure. The pricing page lists a Free plan, paid plans and an Enterprise plan.

## Worth knowing

Single sign-on and SCIM provisioning, along with US and EU routing of data, are features of the Enterprise plan according to the pricing page.
