relax is an Elixir toolset, built on Plug, for building APIs that follow the JSON:API specification at jsonapi.org. Its router and resources handle the requests, and its [documentation on Hex](https://hexdocs.pm/relax/) covers the modules.

## Worth knowing

The README calls the project alpha software, says its APIs are still stabilizing, and describes it as pre 1.0. It states no end of development.
