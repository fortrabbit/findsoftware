Scrivito is a web builder whose homepage now names the product JustRelate Web Builder. It combines a visual editor with live preview and a headless backend that delivers content through a RESTful interface. It supports multilingual projects and runs on serverless architecture as a hosted service. The company behind it is JustRelate Deutschland GmbH, which the imprint describes as a company of the JustRelate Group and gives an address in Berlin.

## Worth knowing

Multi-site management and user management with single sign-on are listed as enterprise features on the pricing page. No self-hosted option is documented on the pages read.
