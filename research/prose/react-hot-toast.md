react-hot-toast is a React library for toast notifications. A call to `toast()` shows a message, and a `Toaster` component, rendered once in the app, draws the notifications it emits.

The library also has a promise helper that shows a loading state and then a success or error message from the result of a promise. A headless hook exported from `react-hot-toast/headless` is available for apps that draw their own notifications.
