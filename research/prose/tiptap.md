Tiptap is an editor framework on top of ProseMirror that runs in the visitor's browser and is installed from npm. The [install guide](https://tiptap.dev/docs/editor/getting-started/install/react) lists integrations for several frameworks and a CDN setup.

## Worth knowing

The core is MIT licensed. The homepage names collaboration and AI agents as paid features, and the [pricing page](https://tiptap.dev/pricing) lists the plans.
