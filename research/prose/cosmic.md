Cosmic is a hosted headless CMS for content teams. Editors work in a web dashboard, and content is delivered through a REST API. Four user roles are defined.

Preview tokens let a site render the latest draft of an object. Localization, webhooks, revision history and automatic backups are offered as add-ons, and SAML single sign-on is available on the Large Workspace and Enterprise plans.

## Worth knowing

The service is operated by Cosmic JS, a Delaware corporation with an office in Plano, Texas. No self-hosting option is recorded.
