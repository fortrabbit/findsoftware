Autumn is billing and entitlements infrastructure that sits between an application and Stripe, aimed at AI and SaaS products. The application asks Autumn what a customer may do and records usage against that customer's balance, while Autumn manages subscription state, credit balances and feature entitlements. The customer's subscriptions and payment details stay in the customer's own Stripe account. The company behind it, Rebase, Inc., does business as Autumn.

## Worth knowing

The welcome page calls Autumn open source and says it can be self-hosted, but the docs link no install guide for a self-hosted server. Until one is documented, this record describes only the hosted service.
