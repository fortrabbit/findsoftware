Budibase is a platform for building internal tools, workflow automations and AI agents. The [Docker Compose guide](https://docs.budibase.com/docs/docker-compose) describes a stack of containers, and the architecture docs list CouchDB as the database service and MinIO as the object store for files, beside a Budibase app service and a worker service.

A published app can be embedded in other webpages as an iframe, and the embed settings restrict which parent sites may frame it. The project's site says Budibase can be self-hosted or run for you on Budibase Cloud.
