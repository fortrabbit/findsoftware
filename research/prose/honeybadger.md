Honeybadger is a hosted service for error tracking and logging in web applications, as its [homepage](https://www.honeybadger.io/) describes. Its SDKs cover Ruby, JavaScript, Python, Elixir and PHP, and for browser pages the JavaScript SDK can be added through a hosted CDN script snippet.

Customers choose between storage in the European Union and storage in the United States, and access can be restricted by user role, team membership or resource. Single sign-on is offered through SAML and SSO, and the plans page lists single-tenant or self-hosted deployments as something to discuss with sales.

## Worth knowing

The client libraries are open source and live in their own repository, and the homepage names no open-source server. A free plan is offered next to paid plans, and the self-hosted route is handled through sales rather than through a public install guide.
