FASTPANEL is a control panel for a Linux server that creates and manages websites, email accounts, databases, SSL certificates and backups. The [install guide](https://kb.fastpanel.direct/quick_start/how-to-install-fastpanel/) installs it with a shell script, and the installer lets the admin choose the MySQL version. PostgreSQL can be installed afterward from the Applications menu. The interface is available in several languages.

## Worth knowing

The panel is distributed under the vendor's license agreement. The Standard License is free and covers the basic functions. The Extended License is paid and adds advanced settings, integrations and priority support.
