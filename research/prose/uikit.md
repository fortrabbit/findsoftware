UIkit ships CSS and JavaScript components that a page includes through its own files, then uses through markup. The project's sources are written in Less, and its JavaScript is bundled and transpiled into ECMAScript 5. The files can be installed with pnpm or yarn, loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, or taken from a zip of the compiled files. The framework supports right-to-left languages. YOOtheme GmbH, a company in Hamburg, Germany, maintains it.

## Worth knowing

The "UIkit Pro" item in the site navigation leads to YOOtheme Pro, a WordPress and Joomla theme and page builder that is a separate product from the framework.
