Friendly Captcha is a hosted CAPTCHA service for websites and apps. A site adds the Friendly Captcha widget to a form, and the widget solves a proof-of-work puzzle for each visitor's device in the background while the visitor fills in the form, so the visitor is not shown a challenge to complete. The service is run by Friendly Captcha GmbH from Germany and states that it uses no cookies and stores no personal data from users.

## Worth knowing

The Starter plan limits requests per month, and paid plans raise those limits and the number of protected domains. A free CAPTCHA is offered for non-commercial websites with low traffic. The Enterprise plan adds a self-hosted endpoint option. The open source integrations for React, Vue and Angular are published separately from the hosted service.
