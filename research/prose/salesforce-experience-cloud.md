Salesforce Experience Cloud is the Salesforce product for building customer and partner portals and sites on the Salesforce platform.

## Worth knowing

The product page at salesforce.com/products/experience-cloud/ now returns 404, so the vendor's site no longer shows the product. The Salesforce Help site renders no text without JavaScript, and the developer documentation answers 403 to the research user agent. Current vendor documentation would show a hosted service; a redirect to another Salesforce product or an end statement would show it discontinued.
