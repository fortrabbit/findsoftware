Vue I18n is the internationalization plugin for Vue.js. The [introduction](https://vue-i18n.intlify.dev/guide/introduction.html) says it is one of the Intlify projects and that it integrates localization features into a Vue.js application.

## Worth knowing

The maintenance page says earlier major versions have reached end of life and are no longer actively maintained, and that the current major continues to be maintained.
