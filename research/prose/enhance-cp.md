Enhance is a hosting control panel for servers, websites, customers and backups. It installs with a shell script from the [installation guide](https://enhance.com/docs/getting-started/installation-guide.html) and runs as management daemons. The web servers it offers are Apache, Nginx, LiteSpeed and OpenLiteSpeed, set per server, and PHP versions are selected per website. Every feature is also available through the REST API.

The FAQ says the frontend is React and TypeScript and that Rust is used under the hood.

## Worth knowing

Enhance is commercial software. Its terms license it rather than sell it, and the pricing page bills per hosted website.
