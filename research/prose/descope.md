Descope is a hosted identity service for customer and agentic identities, and no self-install is recorded for it. An application connects to it through Descope's SDKs, and its sign-up and login journeys are configured as visual workflows. The platform covers SSO over SAML and OIDC and SCIM provisioning. The [migration guides](https://docs.descope.com/migrate) cover moving users over from other identity providers. Descope, Inc. is a Delaware company that states its headquarters are in the United States.

## Worth knowing

Features are sold in plan tiers. Multi-region data residency is listed among the plan features on the pricing page.
