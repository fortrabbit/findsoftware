php-qrcode renders QR Codes through output modules for formats such as SVG, HTML, EPS, PDF and raster images, and decodes them from images with a reader that needs the GD or ImageMagick PHP extension. Its README describes the generator as based on Kazuhiko Arase's implementation and the reader as based on a PHP port of ZXing.

The maintainer's demo page on the project's own site generates QR Codes with the library.
