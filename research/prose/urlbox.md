Urlbox is a hosted service that renders web pages and HTML into screenshots and PDFs through an API. A site or app sends a render request to the API. The [API reference](https://urlbox.com/docs/api) describes the render endpoint, and the [webhooks page](https://urlbox.com/docs/webhooks) describes receiving a notification when a render is generated. Urlbox Ltd operates the service, and its terms name a company registered in England and Wales.

## Worth knowing

The service is offered in US or EU infrastructure. An on-premise deployment is listed on the pricing page and the homepage, but it needs a sales contact, so this record does not treat it as a self-install route.

The API is a JSON-based REST API, and request rates are limited per minute according to the plan.
