Optimizely SaaS CMS is the fully managed cloud edition of Optimizely CMS. Editors build and preview pages in a visual editor, and live preview can be enabled for the site. Content is delivered through Optimizely Graph's GraphQL API, and the content management API provides REST endpoints for basic content operations.

Optimizely North America Inc. maintains the product. Users sign in through Opti ID, which holds system and custom roles, and the CMS delivers content in multiple languages.

## Worth knowing

The self-managed edition of Optimizely CMS is a different product. Optimizely's plans page says every plan is individually packaged, and no prices are published there.
