EasyAdmin is a bundle for Symfony applications that generates an admin panel from PHP configuration, with Twig templates for the views. It is installed into a Symfony project with Composer, and the [documentation](https://symfony.com/bundles/EasyAdminBundle/current/index.html) describes the configuration. The project is maintained by EasyCorp, a company based in Spain, and its source is on GitHub.

EasyCorp also sells EasyAdmin Pro, an edition with features the free bundle lacks. It is distributed under a commercial license described on the [license page](https://easycorp.io/license).

## Worth knowing

The free bundle and the Pro edition are separate products under different licenses.
