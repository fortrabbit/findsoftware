Lemon Squeezy is a hosted service for selling software, digital downloads, subscriptions and license keys. Its pages describe it as the seller's merchant of record and list global tax compliance and fraud prevention among its functions. Sellers get a Supplier Dashboard, and the [API reference](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/api) describes a REST API, with webhooks for asynchronous events and official SDKs for JavaScript and Laravel.

According to the terms, the service is sold through Link, LLC, formerly Lemon Squeezy LLC. Stripe, Inc. acquired the company in 2024.

## Worth knowing

The company's 2026 update presents Stripe Managed Payments, Stripe's merchant-of-record product, as the place it wants Lemon Squeezy users to migrate to.
