LimeSurvey is an open-source survey platform for creating surveys that respondents take through a link or through an embed on the creator's own website. The software is written in PHP and needs one of four databases: MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL or Microsoft SQL Server. It is installed from a zip package or through a command line route. LimeSurvey GmbH of Hamburg, Germany, is the company behind the project.

The platform has a web administration page, multilingual surveys, and single sign-on through LDAP. A RemoteControl web service based on XML-RPC and JSON-RPC lets another application control LimeSurvey. A hosted version, LimeSurvey Cloud, is offered next to the self-hosted Community Edition.

## Worth knowing

ComfortUpdate, named next to the Community Edition on the pricing page, is a paid update service for self-hosted installs. It is not a separate edition with extra features.
