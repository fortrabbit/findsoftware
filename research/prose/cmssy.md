Cmssy is a hosted headless CMS with a visual editor. Editors assemble pages from blocks, and developers render published content in their own Next.js application through a GraphQL delivery API. An AI assistant and an MCP server connect to a workspace and edit its content. The About page names MongoDB as the database.

## Worth knowing

The homepage says the frontend is the user's own: cmssy does not host the site, and the Next.js app is deployed to the user's host. Cart and checkout are listed from the Pro plan up, and single sign-on through OpenID Connect providers is part of the Enterprise plan.
