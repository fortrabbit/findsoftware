kramdown is a Ruby library that parses kramdown, a superset of Markdown, and converts it to HTML. It is written in pure Ruby. Its input formats are kramdown, Markdown, GFM and HTML, and its output formats include HTML, kramdown, LaTeX, and PDF through Prawn.

## Worth knowing
