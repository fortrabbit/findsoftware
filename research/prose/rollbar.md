Rollbar is a hosted error tracking service. Applications send their errors through Rollbar SDKs, and the service groups them and links each one to the code and deploy behind it. Rollbar, Inc. operates it, and its primary data center is in the Iowa region. The pricing page lists SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning.

## Worth knowing

Rollbar Lines is a separate product that runs on the customer's own systems, but a Rollbar team installs it, so it has no public self-install guide.
