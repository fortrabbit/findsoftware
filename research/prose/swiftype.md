Swiftype is a hosted site search service that crawls a website and returns search results for it, with analytics available through an API. The service is owned by Elastic. Its pricing page lists multilingual support among its search features.

## Worth knowing

Swiftype has no installable part, so a site that uses it depends on the hosted service for its search. Customer data is hosted in the United States, and the privacy FAQ says no customer data is hosted within the European Union.
