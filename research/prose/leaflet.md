Leaflet draws interactive, mobile-friendly maps in the visitor's browser. It comes from npm, a CDN or a download, and a static site can serve it as a file. The [plugins page](https://leafletjs.com/plugins.html) lists extensions built on top of it.

## Worth knowing

The newest published version of Leaflet is a prerelease of the next major line. The current stable release belongs to the previous major line.
