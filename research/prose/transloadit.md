Transloadit is a hosted file upload and processing service for images, video, audio and documents, operated by Transloadit-II GmbH in Berlin, Germany. Applications send files and processing instructions to its REST API. SAML single sign-on can be enabled for an Enterprise Workspace. A workspace has account owners and collaborators, and owners have more privileges. Data residency is set by choosing one of three regions.

## Worth knowing

The service runs only in Transloadit's cloud. The open source projects the company lists, such as Uppy and tus, are separate software and are not described here.
