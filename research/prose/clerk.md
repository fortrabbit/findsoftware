Clerk runs authentication and user management as a hosted service, and no self-hosted install is documented. Its security page states that data is hosted on US infrastructure and that regional data residency is not offered. Enterprise single sign-on covers SAML, OIDC and EASIE connections, and users are assigned to predefined Admin and Member roles, with custom roles and permissions on top of them.

## Worth knowing

The client and server SDKs are separate open source packages next to the hosted service. The product documentation is on the [docs site](https://clerk.com/docs).
