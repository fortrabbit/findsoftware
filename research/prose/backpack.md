Backpack for Laravel is a collection of Laravel packages for building custom administration panels, installed with Composer on top of an existing Laravel project or a fresh one. New installs come with one of the Bootstrap-based themes the [getting-started guide](https://backpackforlaravel.com/docs/7.x/getting-started-basics) names.

## Worth knowing

The free package, backpack/crud, is MIT-licensed. Features for more complex cases sit in a separate add-on, Backpack PRO, under a private end-user license agreement, and Backpack DevTools is a further paid product named on the installation page. The [free versus paid comparison](https://backpackforlaravel.com/docs/features-free-vs-paid) marks each feature.
