Cookiebot is a hosted consent management platform for website owners, sold as Cookiebot CMP by Usercentrics A/S, a company registered in Denmark. A site adds the script that its Cookiebot account provides, and the [developer documentation](https://www.cookiebot.com/en/developer/) describes that script and the client-side object it creates in the visitor's browser. Integrations are offered for WordPress and Wix. Role management and a choice of 47+ banner languages are listed on the [pricing page](https://www.cookiebot.com/en/pricing/) as features of the Advanced and Premium plans respectively.

## Worth knowing

The terms say the consent log is stored in a data center with the vendor's cloud provider, Microsoft Ireland Operations.
