Site Search 360 is a hosted site search service offered as software as a service. Its search runs on the vendor's servers, and a JavaScript script that the site includes connects the search box to them.

The company is Zoovu (Germany) GmbH, headquartered in Dresden. The project's own comparison pages name Algolia, AddSearch, Coveo, Google Custom Search, Google Site Search, Swiftype and Yext as alternatives.

## Worth knowing

A free trial is offered, and the pricing page lists plans with monthly prices, which are not recorded here. The pricing, features and FAQ pages read name no single sign-on, user roles, live preview or self-hosted edition.
