Req is a batteries-included HTTP client for Elixir. It decodes response bodies, follows redirects and retries on errors, and its features are built from steps that can be reused, rearranged or replaced with new ones. It is built on top of [Finch](https://github.com/sneako/finch), which is its default adapter, and adapters can be swapped.

Packages that extend it are listed in the related packages section of the README.
