ExpertRec is a hosted AI search and merchandising service for e-commerce and content websites, run by Cloud Infra LLC. Sites connect to it through platform plugins, such as the WordPress plugin published on wordpress.org, or through its search API. Its plans are listed in the pricing section of the homepage.

## Worth knowing

The installable parts are platform connectors. The sources name no server a customer runs, so the site depends on the service for its search.

The fee for the service is the one set on the company's website, and the terms are governed by Delaware law.
