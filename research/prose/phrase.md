Phrase is a hosted localization and translation platform. Source strings stay in a project's codebase, and translations are shipped back into it, driven from an API, a CLI or an MCP server, as the [developer hub](https://developers.phrase.com/) describes.

The company is Phrase a.s., formerly Memsource a.s., a joint stock company under Czech law. The terms place it in the Phrase Group, which Phrase Group Holding s.r.o. controls.

## Worth knowing

The pricing page lists single sign-on and user role access control among its security and access features.
