Crystallize is a hosted headless commerce and product information management service that serves its data through GraphQL APIs. Its web app lets teams control access with roles that set what users can read, create, update and delete, both in the app and through the API. Product data can be multilingual across the product universe. The service is provided by Crystallize AS under its service terms.

The pricing page lists a free tier with pay as you go pricing and a plan with SAML single sign-on.

## Worth knowing

Crystallize is a multi-tenant software-as-a-service platform.
