Loops is a hosted email platform for software companies. Applications manage contacts, campaigns, email content, themes and workflows through its [REST API](https://loops.so/docs/api), and the same platform runs marketing and transactional email from the web app.

## Worth knowing

The service is hosted; the pages read show no self-hosted version. The API is rate limited, with a baseline of 10 requests per second per team. Pricing is by subscribed contact, and the free plan has a send limit.
