BaseHub is a hosted headless CMS with a block editor on its dashboard. A site reads the content through a GraphQL API, and the SDK installs with pnpm as the platform overview shows. Team members hold one of four roles: owner, member, editor or viewer.

The localization guide adds languages with a Variants Block, which GraphQL queries can select by variant. The dashboard offers a preview of work in progress in draft mode.

## Worth knowing

BaseHub has announced that it is joining Vercel. The announcement says the service keeps working as it does today, and that Vercel does not plan to enter the CMS space.
