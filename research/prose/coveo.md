Coveo is a hosted enterprise search platform. It indexes content from many systems into one index and serves search, generative answers and product discovery through headless APIs and the Atomic and Headless libraries. Coveo Solutions Inc., headquartered in Canada, maintains the service.

Content is hosted on AWS in data centers in Canada, the United States, the European Union and Australia, and the customer chooses the region.

## Worth knowing

Single sign-on and role-based access controls are listed on the pricing page. The page offers a free trial, and no free tier is recorded.
