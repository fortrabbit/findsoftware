TCPDF is a PHP library for generating PDF documents and barcodes in application code. It is installed with Composer and maintained by Tecnick.com LTD.

## Worth knowing

The maintainers have deprecated TCPDF. The [README](https://github.com/tecnickcom/TCPDF/blob/main/README.md) says it is in maintenance-only mode and asks new projects to use tc-lib-pdf, its successor package.
