Contentstack is a hosted headless CMS. Content managers author content in a hosted web interface, and the content is delivered through REST and GraphQL APIs. Data is stored in one of seven regions on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, with Europe options on each, and entries can be created in more than 200 languages. The company is Contentstack Inc., based in Austin, Texas.

Live Preview shows entry edits as they are made, before an entry is published. Custom roles are available on the plans that include them, and single sign-on uses SAML 2.0. The documentation describes migrating content from WordPress and from Sitecore.

## Worth knowing

The pages read name no self-hosting option. A free plan is listed on the [pricing page](https://www.contentstack.com/pricing).
