Cleavr is a hosted platform for managing servers and the apps that run on them. It provisions servers at cloud providers the customer connects, including Vultr, DigitalOcean, AWS, UpCloud, Linode and Hetzner, or takes a custom server. It sets up MySQL, PostgreSQL or MariaDB database servers and deploys PHP and Node.js apps to them. The docs at docs.cleavr.io describe the flow.

The service is run by Vistafari LLC. Its privacy notice says personal data is primarily kept in the European Union, in Finland and Germany. The pricing page lists a free trial and paid plans, recorded here only as a link.

## Worth knowing

The platform itself is not open source: the cleavr GitHub organization holds setup scripts for the supported frameworks, not the product. On paid accounts, team members are added and given access to the teams and resources an administrator assigns, and the pages name no roles beyond that. The API is available on a trial and on the business plan.
