Ark UI is a headless component library. The [homepage](https://ark-ui.com/) describes its components as unstyled. It is built on top of Zag.js, as the [about page](https://ark-ui.com/docs/overview/about) states, and ships as separate packages for React, Solid, Vue and Svelte from one repository.

A locale provider sets the locale and direction of the app.

## Worth knowing

Ark Plus sells premium examples for a one-time payment, and the [Plus page](https://ark-ui.com/plus) lists them alongside the open-source library.
