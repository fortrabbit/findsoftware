Recurly is a hosted subscription management and recurring billing platform. Its [homepage](https://recurly.com/) is titled as a recurring billing platform, and sites and apps connect to it through its API and webhooks. The [developer documentation](https://developers.recurly.com/api/) covers the latest version of that API.

The product offers payments through several gateways and email in 30 languages. Teams manage user roles and permissions, and single sign-on is listed on the pricing page without a protocol. The company is Recurly, Inc., which gives a San Francisco address.

## Worth knowing

Recurly is offered only as a hosted service. The company states that the services are hosted and operated in the United States.
