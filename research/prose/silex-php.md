Silex is a PHP micro-framework for developing websites, built on Symfony components and installed with Composer. The project is in maintenance mode: its repository says so, and Symfony's blog post on the end of Silex gives an end of life of June 2018 and points users to Symfony 4 with Flex.

## Worth knowing

The repository is archived on GitHub, and Packagist marks the package abandoned in favor of symfony/flex. The project's domain now redirects to the blog post about the end of Silex.
