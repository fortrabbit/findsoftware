Braid is a design system from SEEK, published as the npm package braid-design-system. Its components are React components.

## Worth knowing

The [README](https://github.com/seek-oss/braid-design-system/blob/master/README.md) words its setup guide as optimised for the sku build tool, and adds: "If your project has a custom build setup, you'll need some extra guidance from project contributors to configure your bundler."
