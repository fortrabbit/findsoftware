Flight is a micro-framework for PHP. It is installed with `composer require flightphp/core`, or from a zip of the repository, and a minimal application is one `index.php` file, as the [documentation](https://docs.flightphp.com/en/v3/) shows.

The project describes itself as the maintained fork of mikecao/flight. The source is in the flightphp organization on GitHub, the copyright line credits Mike Cao and the FlightPHP Team, and the docs keep a list of [plugins](https://docs.flightphp.com/awesome-plugins).
