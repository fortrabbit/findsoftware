Fluent Assertions is a C# library of extension methods for expressing assertions in .NET unit tests. The [homepage](https://fluentassertions.com) names the test frameworks it supports, and the library is installed from NuGet. Dennis Doomen wrote the library first and Jonas Nyrup joined later, and Xceed Software Inc. now supports and maintains it.

## Worth knowing

The current major line is free for open-source projects and non-commercial use, and commercial use requires a paid license. The previous major line stays open source under its earlier terms. The [product page](https://xceed.com/products/unit-testing/fluent-assertions/) sells the commercial license as a subscription.
