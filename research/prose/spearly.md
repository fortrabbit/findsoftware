Spearly CMS is a hosted platform that runs A/B tests on the content of a website. A tag embedded in an existing page adds the product to the site, and editors edit the content in the Spearly admin screen. The homepage says the tag works with WordPress and with any environment where HTML can be edited. The homepage footer names UNIMAL INC. as the operator.

## Worth knowing

The product is a hosted service with no self-host option stated on the pages read, and the operator's location is not stated on them.
