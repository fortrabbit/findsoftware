Upstash Redis is a managed, Redis-compatible database that Upstash runs in its cloud. Its global database option replicates writes from a primary region to read regions. The same company sells other data services, including Vector, QStash, Workflow, Box and Blob.

## Worth knowing

Requests above the plan's maximum request size are rejected with an exception. The free tier is limited to a fixed amount of data and commands per month, and the company offers SAML single sign-on as a security feature listed on its pricing page.
