Locize is a hosted localization management platform for websites and applications. Team members are assigned roles on each project, and the product receives its translations through the Locize CDN or an API. The company behind it, inweso GmbH, is registered in Switzerland, and the service stores personal data primarily in Switzerland and the European Economic Area.

## Worth knowing

Locize is offered only as a hosted service, and no self-hosted option is recorded. Single sign-on works with SAML 2.0 identity providers only; the user-management documentation states that OIDC login and SCIM provisioning are not supported. The vendor's compare page names Lokalise, Crowdin, Localazy and Weblate among the alternatives to Locize.
