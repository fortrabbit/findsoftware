Squiz DXP is a digital experience platform from Squiz for building and running websites and digital services. A command line tool for the platform is published on npm as @squiz/dxp-cli-next.

## Worth knowing

The Squiz product and documentation pages answer 403 to the research user agent, so the deployment model, any headless API and any installable parts are unread. Whether the platform meets the register's criteria is open until a readable product or docs page, or a browser check, settles it.
