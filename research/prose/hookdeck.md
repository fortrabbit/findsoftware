Hookdeck is a hosted service for webhooks in both directions. Its Event Gateway receives inbound webhooks, routes them and queues their delivery, while Outpost sends outbound events from an application to its customers' endpoints. Hookdeck Technologies Inc. runs the service from Montreal, Canada, and its pricing page lists Developer, Team, Growth and Enterprise plans.

## Worth knowing

The open-source sending component, [Outpost](https://hookdeck.com/outpost), can be self-hosted. The hosted service is the Event Gateway and the managed Outpost, and the pricing page lists single sign-on and a read-only user role without naming the plan that carries each one.
