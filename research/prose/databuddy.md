Databuddy is a hosted product analytics platform for websites and apps. Its homepage presents product analytics for startups with a built-in AI analyst, and its terms describe the analytics platform served at app.databuddy.cc. Its pages also list error tracking, Web Vitals, feature flags, short links and uptime monitoring as parts of the same product.

The software is a hosted service, and the record has no public source code. The organization on GitHub that its developer pages link has public repositories for SDKs, plugins and tools, not for the product.

## Worth knowing

Plans are tiered, from a free plan up to Enterprise. SSO by SAML or OIDC is on the Scale plan. The pricing page sets the tiers, and this record lists no prices.
