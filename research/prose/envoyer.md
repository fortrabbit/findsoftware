Envoyer is a hosted deployment service for PHP applications that aims at zero-downtime releases, and it is tuned for Laravel applications. The [documentation](https://docs.envoyer.io/introduction) says it deploys any PHP project to one or several servers, and it integrates with GitHub, Bitbucket and GitLab, including self-hosted GitLab. Deployments can be rolled back, and the service adds application health checks, cron job monitoring and chat notifications. Servers can be imported from Laravel Forge.

The service is offered by Laravel Holdings Inc, and the homepage describes it as the creators of Laravel. The privacy policy states that the company is headquartered in Benton, Arkansas.

## Worth knowing

Envoyer is a hosted service, and the homepage offers a free trial on its paid plans. The documentation says the service is not needed for applications that run Laravel Octane, because Octane already includes zero-downtime deployments.
