Cookies EU banner is a vanilla JavaScript library, written in TypeScript, that shows a cookie consent banner for tracking services such as Google Analytics. The site adds a template element with the banner markup, then loads the script from a package manager or a CDN as the [install guide](https://alex-d.github.io/Cookies-EU-banner/documentation/get-started/) describes. The choice to accept or reject is stored in a cookie, and when the visitor declines the library removes Google Analytics cookies. A headless build leaves the banner's interface to the site.

Alexandre Demode writes and maintains the library, and its presentation site links his Bluesky account.

## Worth knowing

The banner stays hidden from bots and from visitors whose browsers send Do Not Track.
