Open Social is a Drupal distribution for building social communities and intranets, delivered as an install profile on top of Drupal core. The [template project](https://github.com/goalgorilla/social_template/blob/master/README.md) installs it with Composer, and the site is then installed like any other Drupal site.

Open Social B.V., a company based in Amsterdam, maintains the distribution and also sells hosted products, which are separate from it.

## Worth knowing

The repository states GPL-2.0 in the detected LICENSE file and GPL-2.0-or-later in composer.json.
