doc2.site is a hosted service that reads content from Google Docs, Sheets and Drive and publishes it as HTML and JSON. Authors write in Google's tools, and developers build sites from the published content with frameworks such as Next.js, Nuxt.js and Astro. The service has an API reference and a headless API, and a dashboard manages projects. DOC2.SITE LTD operates it.

The product family also includes doc2.live, the delivery service named in the docs, along with doc2.email and doc2.mail for email templates.

## Worth knowing

Content stays in Google Drive, so the service depends on the Google accounts that hold the documents, and subscribing to a plan requires a Google account. A private workspace keeps published content visible only to its publisher, and a preview workspace is open to anyone with its unique link.
