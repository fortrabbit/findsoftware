Elixir here is the main language of the repository. Elixir runs on the [Erlang VM](/runtimes/beam/), which handles many concurrent connections in lightweight processes, and its packages come from Hex. Contributing to or extending software in this group means writing Elixir. Applications can ship as releases that bundle the runtime, so operating them does not always require Elixir on the server. A record carries this value when Elixir makes up most of the codebase. Phoenix and Plausible Analytics are examples.
