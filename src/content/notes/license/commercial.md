---
description: Software distributed under a proprietary license, typically requiring a purchase or subscription to use it.
lead: Proprietary terms, usually a paid license.
---

Commercial means the software is offered under a proprietary license set by its vendor, and using it in production requires accepting those terms, usually by buying a license or subscription. The code may be delivered as source or as a package, but the license grants no general right to redistribute or change it. A free trial or free tier can exist. A record holds this value when the project's license and pricing pages describe proprietary terms for the software as a whole. Where the source is public with restrictions, the record is [source available](/license/source-available/).
