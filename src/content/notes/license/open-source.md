description: Software released in full under a license approved as open source, free to use, change and redistribute.
lead: The whole codebase under an open-source license.
---

Open source here means the complete software is published under a license that meets the Open Source Definition, such as MIT, GPL or Apache 2.0. Anyone may use it for any purpose, change it and share the changes, within the license's conditions. A company may still sell support, hosting or add-ons. A record holds this value when every part needed to run the software is under such a license. When some features sit under different terms, the record is [open core](/license/open-core/).
