description: Software created and maintained mainly by one person.
lead: One person leads it.
---

Individual-maintained means one person created the project and holds the final say over its releases and direction, even when others contribute. Many widely used libraries and tools work this way, sometimes funded through sponsorships or a paid product alongside. Decisions can be quick and consistent, and continuity depends on that person. A record holds this value when the repository belongs to a personal account or the project names one person as its author and lead, with no company or foundation behind it.
