---
description: Software maintained under a nonprofit foundation that holds its assets and sets its governance.
lead: A nonprofit holds the project.
---

Foundation-maintained means a nonprofit organization holds the project's trademark, code or funds and sets out how decisions are made, often with an elected board or a steering group. Development is done by volunteers, sponsors' employees or staff paid from donations. No single company controls it, and governance rules are usually public. A record holds this value when the project's site names a foundation as its owner or home, whether a foundation of its own, as with [Django](/django/), or an umbrella foundation hosting many projects.
