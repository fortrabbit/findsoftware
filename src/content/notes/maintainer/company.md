---
description: Software maintained by a company that employs its core developers or owns the project.
lead: A business owns and develops it.
---

Company-maintained means a business owns the project or employs the people who decide its releases, and usually funds development from licenses, paid editions, hosting or services. The software may still be open source and take outside contributions. Direction follows the company's plans, and a change of ownership or strategy can change the project. A record holds this value when the project's site or repository names a company as owner or maintainer, including a large company maintaining a project it uses internally.
