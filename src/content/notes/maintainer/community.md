---
description: Software maintained by a group of volunteers or contributors with no company or foundation owning it.
lead: A group of contributors keeps it going.
---

Community-maintained means a group of contributors, usually organized around a GitHub organization, shares the work and the decisions, without a company or foundation behind the project. Funding, where it exists, comes from donations or sponsorship platforms. Direction comes from the active maintainers, and continuity depends on enough of them staying involved. A record holds this value when the project presents itself as run by a team of contributors and no [company](/maintainer/company/) or [foundation](/maintainer/foundation/) is named as owner.
