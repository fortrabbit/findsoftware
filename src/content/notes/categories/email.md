---
description: Software that sends newsletters, transactional email or notifications for a site or app.
lead: Newsletters, app email and notifications.
---

This category covers messages a site sends: newsletter and mailing-list managers that keep subscriber lists and send campaigns, publishing tools with a built-in newsletter, services that send transactional email or push notifications from an application, and development tools that catch outgoing mail for testing. Sending at volume usually goes through an SMTP provider configured in the software. A record carries this category when sending messages is a main function. listmonk and Mailpit are examples.
