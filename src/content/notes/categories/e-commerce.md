description: Software for running an online store, from the product catalog to checkout and orders.
lead: Products, cart and checkout.
---

E-commerce software manages a product catalog, a cart and checkout, payments through provider integrations, and the orders that follow. Some ship a complete storefront, others are headless and deliver the catalog over an API to a separate front end. A plugin that adds a shop to another system gets its own record, with the parent named under "Built on". A record carries this category when selling goods or services is what the software is for. PrestaShop and Medusa are examples.
