---
description: Software for discussion around a site, from forums and social networks to comment threads under pages.
lead: Discussion, on your own server.
---

Community software lets visitors talk to each other and to the site. Forums organize threads by topic, social and fediverse servers connect user profiles and feeds, and comment systems add a discussion thread under each page through an embedded script, which gives comments to sites built with a [static site generator](/categories/ssg/). Each stores posts on its own server with moderation and sign-in options. A record carries this category when discussion is its main job. Remark42 is an example.
