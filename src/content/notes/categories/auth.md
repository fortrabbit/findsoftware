---
description: Software that handles user login, identity and access for applications.
lead: Login and identity.
---

Authentication software manages users and sign-in: passwords, single sign-on over protocols such as OpenID Connect or SAML, multi-factor login and permissions. Applications hand login over to it instead of building their own. A record carries this category when identity and access are a main function, as for a standalone identity provider or a [backend platform](/categories/backend/) with built-in auth. Keycloak and authentik are examples.
