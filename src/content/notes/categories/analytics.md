description: Software that records how a site is used or whether it works, from visitor statistics to error tracking and uptime checks.
lead: Visits, errors and uptime, on your own install.
---

This category covers software that watches a running site. Web analytics tools collect page views and events through a script or server logs and report on traffic. Error trackers take reports from an SDK inside the application, and uptime checkers poll the site from outside and alert someone when it stops answering. The installable ones keep that data on the operator's own server. A record carries this category when measuring or watching a site is its purpose. Many also offer a hosted version beside the self-hosted one. Matomo, Sentry and Uptime Kuma are examples.
