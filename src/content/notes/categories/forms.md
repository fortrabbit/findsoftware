description: Software for building forms and surveys and collecting the responses.
lead: Forms and surveys on your own server.
---

Form and survey software lets someone build a form without writing code, embed it in a site or app or link to it, and collect and review the responses. Some add logic between questions, targeting rules or exports to other tools. A record carries this category when collecting input from visitors is its main job, not when a CMS includes a contact form feature. Formbricks is an example.
