Athos Commerce is a hosted AI site search service for online retailers, operated by B7 Interactive, LLC under the Athos Commerce name in North America. The site search product was sold as Searchspring, and the console was renamed to Athos Commerce. The search covers 31 languages.

## Worth knowing

The service runs on the vendor's cloud infrastructure, so a shop depends on the vendor for search to work. The record finds no self-hosted option. The pricing page lists plans, and the record holds no prices.
