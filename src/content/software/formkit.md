FormKit is a form framework for React and Vue that builds inputs, validation and nested form data from one component. Its framework-agnostic core is separate from the React and Vue bindings.

## Worth knowing

FormKit Pro is a paid edition with premium inputs such as autocomplete, datepicker and rating, separate from the free core. The Pro page says the inputs are free during development and paid once a project ships.
