Uniform is a hosted digital experience platform that combines a headless CMS, visual editing, personalization and A/B testing, and composes content from other systems. Editors work in a hosted dashboard with live preview, and sites read content through REST and GraphQL APIs. Uniform Systems, Inc. runs the service and offers data regions in the US and the EU.

Single sign-on is documented with SAML 2.0 for an identity provider, and inbound SCIM 2.0 syncs users and groups. Access is controlled with role-based access control.

## Worth knowing

The service is sold as a subscription under a master agreement that grants SaaS use rights, with no free tier; a trial account is available on request. The Siphon migration toolkit is licensed and provided separately from the platform subscription.
