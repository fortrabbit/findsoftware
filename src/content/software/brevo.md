Brevo is a hosted email platform operated by Brevo SAS, a French company with its registered office in Paris. The [pricing page](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) says every plan includes transactional email through RESTful APIs, SMTP and outbound webhooks, and the [developer documentation](https://developers.brevo.com) covers the API. The [rate limits page](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/api-limits) describes three tiers of API limits. According to the [security page](https://www.brevo.com/security/), its servers are located in the EU.

## Worth knowing

Plans are monthly and set by email volume, according to the pricing page. The free plan is described there as free forever with no credit card needed. The pricing page lists SSO and SAML among its features.
