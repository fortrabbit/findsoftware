Pipedream is a hosted integration platform. Its Connect toolkit lets an app or an AI agent add thousands of third-party integrations, as the [Connect documentation](https://pipedream.com/docs/connect) describes. A separate workflow feature, documented in the same docs, builds automations.

## Worth knowing

The integrations run through the vendor's hosted service, which the privacy and security documentation places on Amazon Web Services in one region. Single sign-on and SCIM are listed on the Business tier of the pricing page.
