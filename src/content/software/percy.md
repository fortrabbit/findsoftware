Percy is a hosted visual testing and review service at percy.io, part of BrowserStack. Teams send snapshots from their builds through the Percy command-line client, a package that uploads them to the service. The CLI can also approve, unapprove, reject or delete builds on percy.io from the command line.

## Worth knowing

The pricing page lists monthly screenshot quotas for its plans. The client is a separate package from the hosted service it uploads to.
