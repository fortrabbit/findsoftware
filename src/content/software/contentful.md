Contentful is a hosted content platform that stores structured content and delivers it through REST and GraphQL APIs. Editors work in a hosted web app. Its official JavaScript packages cover reading from the Content Delivery and Preview APIs, writing through the Content Management API, and a command-line tool. The CLI can be configured for the EU endpoints, and starter projects for Next.js cover blog, marketing and ecommerce sites.

## Worth knowing

The platform runs as a hosted service, and its open-source repositories hold the client libraries and starters.
