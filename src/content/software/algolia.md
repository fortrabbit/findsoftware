Algolia is a hosted AI search and retrieval platform, operated by Algolia, Inc. Its terms name Algolia SAS, a French company, as the contracting party for subscribers in Europe. Search is served through APIs and client libraries, and the [documentation](https://www.algolia.com/doc/guides/getting-started/what-is-algolia/) describes an Algolia dashboard for tuning relevance. Data is held in the company's data centers, which the homepage lists by region.

Single sign-on is offered on the Elevate plan, according to the pricing page.
