AdminKit is an admin and dashboard template built on Bootstrap 5. Its pages and styles are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: a site can use the compiled files as they are, or change the source and rebuild with Webpack, which needs Node.js on the developer's machine. The terms page names Bootlab B.V. as the company the product belongs to.

## Worth knowing

The free edition is MIT-licensed. More pages, plugins, dashboards and color schemes are in the PRO edition, which the [pricing page](https://adminkit.io/pricing/) sells under a license chosen by team size.
