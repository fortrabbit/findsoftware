Inkeep is a hosted platform, run by Inkeep, Inc., for AI assistants and agents that answer questions from a company's knowledge sources. Its search product, Unified AI Search, is labeled Managed RAG on the pricing page. The [documentation](https://docs.inkeep.com/) describes the platform.

The company's [pricing page](https://docs.inkeep.com/pricing) splits the product into an open-source column and an Enterprise column, and its Enterprise list names Unified AI Search (Managed RAG).

## Worth knowing

The Enterprise plan is a managed platform with dedicated support, so the search runs on the vendor's side of the service. This record covers the hosted search product.
