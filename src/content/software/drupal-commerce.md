Drupal Commerce is a set of modules that adds online store features to a Drupal site. It is installed with Composer into a Drupal project and published on drupal.org, where Centarro is named as its sponsor and maintainer.

## Worth knowing

The drupal.org project page says the site operator is responsible for protecting merchants and customers from online payment fraud, and points to a PCI compliance white paper.
