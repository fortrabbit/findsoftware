OpenObserve is an observability platform for logs, metrics and traces. It stores its data in Parquet files and is written in Rust, and it ships as a single binary, a Docker image or a Kubernetes manifest. A hosted service, OpenObserve Cloud, is offered beside the self-hosted install, and the [documentation](https://openobserve.ai/docs/) covers installation and building dashboards.

## Worth knowing

The open source edition is licensed under AGPL-3.0. The Enterprise edition is licensed under a commercial agreement and adds single sign-on and custom roles, according to the project's README.

The record is a stub because the project describes itself as an observability platform for running infrastructure, which leaves it unclear whether it is a tool for building or extending a site.
