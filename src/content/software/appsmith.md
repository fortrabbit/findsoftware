Appsmith is an open source low-code platform for building internal tools, admin panels and dashboards, published by Appsmith, Inc. Its [Docker guide](https://docs.appsmith.com/getting-started/setup/installation-guides/docker) starts the product with Docker Compose, and the Kubernetes and AWS machine image guides cover the other two self-installed routes. The default Docker Compose setup runs MongoDB, Redis and PostgreSQL inside the Appsmith container. A hosted Appsmith Cloud is offered beside the self-installed product.

## Worth knowing

Two editions exist. The Community Edition is licensed under Apache 2.0. The Commercial edition, which the Docker guide recommends, offers a free plan and an upgrade to a paid plan. Its Enterprise Edition needs an enterprise key and falls under the Appsmith Subscription Agreement.
