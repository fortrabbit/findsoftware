Amazon SES is the email service of Amazon Web Services. Applications send email through the SES SMTP interface, the SES API or an AWS SDK, and the service also receives email. The developer guide describes marketing, transactional and newsletter email as uses, and it links the service to Amazon Simple Notification Service for bounce and complaint notifications and to AWS Identity and Access Management for access control. The operator, Amazon Web Services, Inc., is located in the United States.

## Worth knowing

Amazon SES runs as a hosted service in several AWS Regions. The SDK packages that call it are separate projects in their own repositories.
