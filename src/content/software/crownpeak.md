Crownpeak is the name behind the FirstSpirit CMS and related digital experience products. Its pages describe a hybrid-headless CMS: the content repository is separated from the presentation layer, and the pages say the architecture supports static, dynamic, headless or hybrid deployment. Its German entity describes itself as a Rezolve Ai company, and the documentation is at [docs.firstspirit.com](https://docs.firstspirit.com).

## Worth knowing

Crownpeak calls itself a SaaS provider on its legal page, and no installation page for the CMS itself exists. The old crownpeak.com address redirects to the Rezolve site and FirstSpirit's own site is the product site, so the record waits as a stub until it is clear which name it belongs under.
