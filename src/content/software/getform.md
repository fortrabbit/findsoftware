Forminit is a hosted headless form backend API, which was named Getform.io until the company renamed it. A site or app posts its form submissions to a Forminit endpoint, and the service validates, stores and forwards them, with file uploads, email notifications, webhooks and integrations such as Google Sheets and Slack. The help center has quickstarts for HTML, React, Next.js, Astro and other stacks. UXPLUS LTD operates the service from a registered office in London.

## Worth knowing

The company says the renamed service is the same service, with the same accounts and data. Application data is hosted in the AWS Ireland region. A comparison page on the company's site describes Forminit as an alternative to Formspree. The free plan is limited to a number of submissions per month.
