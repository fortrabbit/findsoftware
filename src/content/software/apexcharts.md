ApexCharts is a JavaScript charting library that draws its charts with SVG for websites and web apps. A project installs the npm package apexcharts or includes the library from a CDN.

## Worth knowing

The library is offered under two licenses. The community license is free only for organizations under a revenue, budget or funding threshold, and the paid SKUs named Pro, Premium and OEM fall under the commercial license.
