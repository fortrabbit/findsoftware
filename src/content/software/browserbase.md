Browserbase is a hosted platform that runs cloud browsers, along with search and fetch, for AI agents and applications, reached through an API. The [documentation](https://docs.browserbase.com) covers the platform. Browserbase, Inc. is a Delaware corporation according to its [terms of service](https://www.browserbase.com/terms-of-service).

The [pricing page](https://www.browserbase.com/pricing) names the regions US East, US West, EU Central and the UK, with Asia Pacific available on request for enterprise customers. Its FAQ says the platform is cloud-first and offers no on-prem deployment, but VPC connectivity for secure integration.

## Worth knowing

Plans are monthly, and the pricing page says each plan scales past its included usage and is billed at the plan's rate. The page lists SSO as available on the Enterprise plan. The homepage presents Stagehand and the Browse CLI as open-source products; they are separate from the hosted platform.
