Agility CMS is a hosted headless content management system from Agility Inc. Editors build and change pages in a visual editor, the Web Studio, and edit them in context. Developers read content through the [Fetch API](https://agilitycms.com/docs), which is served over a CDN, and the homepage names REST and GraphQL APIs for building on Next.js, Nuxt, Gatsby, Astro and .NET.

One Agility instance can hold content for several websites, brands and regions, and it supports multilingual content. The platform has built-in and custom roles.

## Worth knowing

Agility CMS is a multi-tenant SaaS platform and the pricing page says it cannot be installed on-premises. Single sign-on is listed on the Enterprise plan only, and the choice of data center location is listed for Enterprise.
