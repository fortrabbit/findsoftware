Foxy is a hosted shopping cart and checkout. A site adds it by placing a link, button or form on a page, and the sale runs through Foxy's hosted cart and checkout. The [API documentation](https://api.foxycart.com/docs) describes a hypermedia-driven REST API, carts and checkout pages are customized with Twig templates, and one login can manage several stores. The company behind it is FoxyCart.com, LLC, and its [help center](https://www.foxy.io/help) holds the product documentation.

## Worth knowing

Foxy's hosted cart and checkout are a service of the company, and the admin provides lightweight order management.
