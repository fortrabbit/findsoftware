Better Stack is a hosted observability platform. Applications can send their logs to its HTTP API, and its documentation also describes ingestion through OpenTelemetry and its own collector. The homepage also sells incident management, status pages, uptime monitoring and an AI SRE feature. The service is operated by Better Stack, Inc., a Delaware corporation.

## Worth knowing

Data is stored in EU regions by default, according to the security page. Enterprise accounts can use SAML single sign-on and role-based access control,.
