Liveblocks is a hosted realtime service for multiplayer apps and AI agents, covering shared data sync, presence, comments and notifications. The [documentation](https://liveblocks.io/docs) names a React client package for connecting an application, and a [REST API](https://liveblocks.io/docs/api-reference/rest-api-endpoints) is available for server-side calls. Liveblocks, Inc. runs the service and first launched the product in 2021.

## Worth knowing

The pricing page lists self-hosting as a paid add-on on the Enterprise plan. No self-install guide was found, so this record describes the hosted service only.
