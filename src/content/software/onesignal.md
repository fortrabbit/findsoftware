OneSignal is a hosted messaging service. An app adds a client SDK, and the service sends push notifications, emails, in-app messages and SMS to the app's users, with campaigns built in a dashboard or sent through an API. Journeys, segmentation and analytics are part of the same platform.

The docs name no self-hosted version. Its client SDKs cover Android, iOS, Flutter, React Native, Expo, Unity and the web.

## Worth knowing

Plans are listed on the pricing page. The free plan is one of them, and the higher plans are sold through a sales contact. Two-factor authentication and audit logs are listed on the higher plans.
