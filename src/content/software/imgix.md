imgix is a hosted service that transforms, optimizes and delivers images and video. It connects to existing storage sources, such as Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage and Cloudflare R2, and transforms files as they are requested through parameters on the image URL. The homepage describes adding text, badges and logos to an image from its URL. Imgix, Inc. operates the service under its [terms](https://www.imgix.com/terms). The setup guide covers sources and accounts, and the documentation holds the rest of the reference.

## Worth knowing

Single sign-on is offered to enterprise customers through an identity provider; the setup page does not name the protocol. Account access is set by role permissions, which the [user management page](https://docs.imgix.com/en-US/getting-started/setup/account-setup-and-analytics/login-and-user-management) lists as Account Admin, Billing and Asset Manager Edit.
