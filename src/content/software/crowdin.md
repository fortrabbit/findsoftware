Crowdin is a hosted localization platform for managing translation work. Its homepage describes it as an AI-powered localization platform for teams and businesses. The [developer portal](https://developer.crowdin.com/) documents a REST API and a GraphQL API, and it lists SDKs for Android, iOS, Flutter and the web. The company is Crowdin OÜ, which the privacy policy registers in Estonia, and the About page dates the company's founding to 2009.

## Worth knowing

Crowdin is offered only as a hosted service: the on-premise and self-hosted paths named on its site do not resolve, and no page describes installing Crowdin on a server of one's own. Data residency in the US or the EU and ISO 27001 are listed on the Enterprise page, and the security page states GDPR and HIPAA compliance.
