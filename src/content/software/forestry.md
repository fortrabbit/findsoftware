Forestry is a content management system for websites whose content is managed in repositories. Its own web address now redirects to tina.io, where the page for Forestry.io names TinaCMS as its successor and says TinaCMS is now maintained by SSW.

## Worth knowing

The Forestry product pages are no longer served, so how it was installed and hosted cannot be read from its own site. The tina.io page describes a migration guide for moving a Forestry site to TinaCMS.
