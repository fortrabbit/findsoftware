Centrifugo is a self-hosted real-time messaging server. It holds client connections over WebSocket and other transports and delivers published messages to browser and app clients. Several Centrifugo nodes can share connections through a broker such as Redis, PostgreSQL or NATS.

## Worth knowing

The homepage names Centrifugo PRO, a separate paid offering. This record does not cover its terms.
