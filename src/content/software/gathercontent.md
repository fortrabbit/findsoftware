GatherContent is a hosted content workflow service with a REST API. Its API reference at docs.gathercontent.com covers items, templates, folders, files and webhooks, and states that the API is based on REST.

Bynder acquired GatherContent in 2022 and has renamed the product Content Workflow by Bynder. The record is a stub: the old domain now redirects to a Bynder page, and the notes give what would settle whether the product is listed under its new name.
