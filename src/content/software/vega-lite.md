Vega-Lite is a high-level grammar of interactive graphics, built on [Vega](https://vega.github.io/vega/): its compiler turns a concise, declarative JSON specification into a Vega specification. The [embed guide](https://vega.github.io/vega-lite/usage/embed.html) covers installing it for a web page, from npm, a CDN or a download.

The University of Washington Interactive Data Lab leads development, and the project posts updates on Bluesky.
