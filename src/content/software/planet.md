Planet is a native macOS app, written in Swift, for building websites and blogs on a Mac and publishing them to IPFS. Its homepage offers a download for Mac, and the source code can also be built by the user.

## Worth knowing

The FAQ says that with a pinning service the traffic is served by the provider's servers, so the user does not need to keep their Mac on. The README describes the app as being at an early stage.
