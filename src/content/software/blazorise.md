Blazorise is a component library for Blazor, the .NET framework for web UI. Its components are used in Blazor apps through the core NuGet package and a provider package for the CSS framework the app chooses, such as Bootstrap or Tailwind, plus an icon package. The company Megabit d.o.o. develops and maintains it, and its documentation, demos and paid plans are on the same website.

## Worth knowing

The license page calls the library source-available under the Blazorise Software License Agreement. The Community License is free for eligible individual developers, and the pricing page says eligibility is confirmed through LinkedIn. The pricing FAQ says companies, government entities, agencies and other organizations need a commercial license. Commercial plans (Professional, Enterprise and Enterprise Plus) add tools, components and support.
