Aceternity UI is a React component library of animated components, blocks and templates, first released in 2024. The site describes three ways to use them: copy and paste, the shadcn CLI against the project's own registry, and an MCP registry that an AI agent can query. Premium blocks, templates and pages are sold as paid plans.

## Worth knowing

The terms reserve all intellectual property rights, and the Aceternity License covers items bought or downloaded on the site, so the material is under a commercial license.
