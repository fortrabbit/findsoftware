Flamelink is a hosted content interface for Google Firebase projects. A customer connects a Firebase project to Flamelink, defines content schemas in Flamelink's web interface, and editors add and publish content there rather than in the Firebase console. The content itself is stored in the customer's Firebase project, in Cloud Firestore or the Realtime Database, and the service is operated by Flamelink B.V.

The homepage lists multiple languages, user permissions and roles, and webhooks as features.

## Worth knowing

Flamelink is a hosted service that works on a Firebase project the customer provides. The pricing page sets plan limits on CMS users, webhooks, environments, languages and schemas, and states that every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial.
