Basin is a hosted form backend. An HTML form posts to a Basin endpoint, and the submissions are handled by the service and configured in its dashboard. A JavaScript library, BasinJS, supports custom form experiences, and webhooks and connected tools such as Zapier, Slack and Google Sheets receive the data. A REST API gives programmatic access to the form data. The service is operated by Moonshot Ventures Inc. from Canada.

## Worth knowing

Submissions are held by the hosted service, so the form's data depends on Basin and on the limits of the plan in use; the pricing page lists a free plan next to paid plans. Team members can be added with collaborator, editor or admin access.
