Statsig is a hosted platform for feature flags, experiments and product analytics. It is used through its SDKs and [docs](https://docs.statsig.com), and Statsig runs the product as a service. The homepage says Statsig is part of the Amplitude family, and the terms name Amplitude, Inc. as the contracting party. The [pricing page](https://www.statsig.com/pricing) lists single sign-on through SAML and OAuth, SCIM user provisioning, role-based access control and hosting in the EU.

## Worth knowing

No self-hosting guide was found on the project's own site, so the record is listed as a hosted service. The SDKs are open source, but the service they call is hosted.
