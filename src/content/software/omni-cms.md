Modern Campus CMS is a content management system for higher education institutions' websites, sold with the vendor's SaaS hosting by Modern Campus USA Inc. Editors create and edit pages in WYSIWYG and drag-and-drop editors, and a low-code Layout Builder generates templates for staff who do not write code. The product page also mentions APIs for extending the CMS.

## Worth knowing

The product runs on the vendor's hosting, so a site on it depends on Modern Campus. The vendor's terms of use page returned not-found, so no license terms were read.
