Luxon is a JavaScript library for working with dates and times. Its [README](https://github.com/moment/luxon/blob/master/README.md) lists DateTime, Duration and Interval types, parsing and formatting for common and custom formats, and native time zone and Intl support with no locale or time zone files. Locales come from the native Intl API.

The documentation includes a [guide for Moment users](https://github.com/moment/luxon/blob/master/docs/moment.md), which names Moment.js as the project Luxon borrows ideas from.
