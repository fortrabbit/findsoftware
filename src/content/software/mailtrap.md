Mailtrap is a hosted email API, SMTP and email sandbox service for testing and sending email, run by Railsware Products Studio LLC. The [documentation](https://docs.mailtrap.io/) describes the platform as email infrastructure for developers, and its [security page](https://mailtrap.io/security-and-compliance/) says customer data is hosted in US data centers. Mailtrap's own pages present it as an alternative to Mailgun and Amazon SES.

## Worth knowing

Mail goes through the vendor's hosted infrastructure, so nothing is installed by the user. Single sign-on through the customer's identity provider is listed for the Enterprise plan.
