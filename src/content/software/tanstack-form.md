TanStack Form manages the state of a form in TypeScript or JavaScript. It is headless, so the application supplies the markup and styles, and the core package has no tie to one framework. Adapters connect the core to React, Vue, Angular, Solid, Lit and Svelte, and separate packages cover the meta-frameworks TanStack Start, Next.js and Remix. The [installation guide](https://tanstack.com/form/latest/docs/installation) asks you to install the adapter for your framework with your preferred package manager.

## Worth knowing

The [comparison page](https://tanstack.com/form/latest/docs/comparison) sets the library against Formik, Redux Form, React Hook Form and Final Form, and it says its table is under construction and may not be accurate. Older browsers need a transpiled copy of the library, as the guide notes.
