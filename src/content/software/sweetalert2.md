SweetAlert2 replaces the browser's popup boxes with responsive, accessible dialogs and has no dependencies. It is a fork of the SweetAlert package, maintained in its own organization and repository.

The package installs from npm, and a page can also load it from the jsDelivr CDN with a script tag. Separate packages integrate it with React, Angular and Laravel.
