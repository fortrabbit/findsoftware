SearchStax is a hosted service that runs Apache Solr search infrastructure for its customers. Its Managed Search product hosts Solr for customers, and its Site Search product is a search service for websites. SearchStax, Inc. is based in California and maintains the service.

## Worth knowing

The search index runs on SearchStax's infrastructure, which the dedicated plans deploy on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, and the record finds no self-hosted option. Its private cloud deployment is provided by the vendor in a separate Virtual Private Cloud, not installed by the customer.
