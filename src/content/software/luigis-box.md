Luigi's Box is a hosted search and product discovery service for e-commerce stores. The [vendor's site](https://www.luigisbox.com/) describes it as an AI-powered search and product discovery suite, and the [documentation](https://docs.luigisbox.com/) covers integrating and customizing it. The vendor names platform integrations for stores on Shopify and WooCommerce, among others, and a REST API for the product features.

Luigi's Box, s.r.o. operates the service from Bratislava, Slovakia, and gives 2016 as the year the service launched. Its [comparison page](https://www.luigisbox.com/algolia-alternative/) names Algolia as the alternative it compares itself with.

## Worth knowing

Pricing is quote-based and measured in units of usage, and the [pricing page](https://www.luigisbox.com/pricing/) says every plan includes the full feature set. The terms name a No-Code Self-Service Integration module as a way to integrate the hosted service.
