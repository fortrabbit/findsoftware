Axiom is a hosted data platform that collects, stores and analyzes event and telemetry data. The platform has a web Console and takes traces, logs and metrics over OpenTelemetry (OTLP). Single sign-on through SAML and role-based access control are offered as add-ons on the Axiom Cloud plan.

## Worth knowing

Axiom runs only as Axiom Cloud, hosted by Axiom in the United States, and no self-hosted version is documented. A free Personal plan is offered alongside the paid plan, and the [pricing page](https://axiom.co/pricing) lists the plans.
