Mollie is a hosted payment platform for online and in-person payments. Websites and apps accept payments by calling its API, as the [developer documentation](https://docs.mollie.com/) describes, and the API has a test mode with its own keys. The company behind it is Mollie B.V., based in Amsterdam, and the payment products cover Europe and the UK.

## Worth knowing

The service is hosted and cannot be installed. The repository mollie/mollie-api-node is described on its own page as a community Node client, so it is a separate project from the service.
