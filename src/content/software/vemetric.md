Vemetric is a web and product analytics platform offered as a hosted service. Its docs list SDKs for HTML script, JavaScript, React, Astro, Node.js, PHP, Python and Go, and a REST API.

## Worth knowing

The platform's source is public under AGPL-3.0, but the project documents no self-hosted installation. Its FAQ says a self-hostable Community Edition is planned and not yet available.

The servers are in the EU, and the free plan has a monthly event allowance.
