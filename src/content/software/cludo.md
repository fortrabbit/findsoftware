Cludo is a hosted site search service for websites, run from the company's cloud, with AI Summary, AI Chat and AI Mode as optional features. Its dashboard, MyCludo, is where marketing and web teams manage layout, banners, quicklinks and analytics. The developer pages describe a REST API for search and autocomplete requests, and the search index is filled by pushing raw data or by a Cludo crawler.

Cludo ApS, based in Copenhagen, maintains the service and offers hosting in the EU and the US.

## Worth knowing

The AI features are premium add-ons with separate pricing. No self-hosted version is offered: the FAQ states that Cludo is cloud-based and needs no server-side installation.
