AppSignal is a hosted error tracking and application performance monitoring service, provided by AppSignal B.V. from the Netherlands. Applications connect through SDKs for Ruby, Elixir, Node.js, JavaScript, Python, Go, Java, PHP and Rust, and setup is documented in the [docs](https://docs.appsignal.com/). The pricing page lists a free plan beside paid plans.

## Worth knowing

Single sign-on is sold as the SAML SSO Business Add-On.
