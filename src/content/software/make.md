Make is a hosted visual automation platform for building workflows and AI agents that connect business apps. Workflows connect integrations with other services, and the [developer hub](https://developers.make.com/) documents a Make API, a CLI, an SDK and custom apps for building new integrations. Make is part of Celonis, and Celonis, Inc. is named as the copyright holder on its site.

## Worth knowing

The service is hosted and cannot be installed. Single sign-on through the customer's own identity provider and custom roles are listed for Enterprise plans.
