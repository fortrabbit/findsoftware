ImageKit is a hosted service for image and video delivery, with transformations applied through parameters in its delivery URLs, and a digital asset management product alongside it. Customers integrate through the ImageKit API and the SDKs the vendor publishes, and media is delivered from the service's own URL endpoints. ImageKit Private Limited operates the service under its [terms](https://imagekit.io/terms/), and ImageKit Inc. is the contracting entity for standard plans. Signed URLs with expiry and private images restrict access, as the [security and trust page](https://imagekit.io/security-and-trust/) describes.

## Worth knowing

The service is offered as a hosted product only, and the pages read describe no self-hosted option. Plans range from a free tier to paid tiers, and the enterprise tier lists single sign-on. Where data is processed depends on the processing region the customer chooses, according to the [GDPR page](https://imagekit.io/gdpr/).
