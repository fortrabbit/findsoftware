Auth0 is a hosted identity service. An application sends its users to Auth0 for login and reads the result through its APIs and SDKs, so nothing is installed on the application's own server. The service speaks [OpenID Connect and OAuth 2.0](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/protocols/openid-connect-protocol), SAML and WebAuthn, and it accepts inbound SCIM from selected enterprise identity providers. Auth0, LLC is wholly owned by Okta, Inc., which publishes the privacy policy that names both.

## Worth knowing

The service has no self-install path. Its [pricing page](https://auth0.com/pricing) prices plans by monthly active users, and a Free plan covers up to a fixed number of them. Role-based access control per organization is listed from the Essentials plan up. The pricing page also describes private deployments, which are dedicated and run by the vendor on AWS or Azure.
