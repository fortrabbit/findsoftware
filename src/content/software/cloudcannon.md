CloudCannon is a hosted, Git-based content management system. It connects to a site's Git repository, builds the static site with every push, and lets editors change pages visually in a live, hosted preview they can share. Content stays versioned in the repository. CloudCannon builds and hosts the site, or the site can be hosted elsewhere.

The company is CloudCannon Limited, incorporated in New Zealand. Its open source tools for static sites, listed on its [open source page](https://cloudcannon.com/open-source/), are separate projects and not the CMS itself.

## Worth knowing

SSO through SAML is listed among the pricing plan features, and custom user roles appear on the [enterprise page](https://cloudcannon.com/enterprise/).
