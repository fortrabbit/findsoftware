iubenda is a hosted compliance service from iubenda S.r.l., an Italian company that is part of the team.blue group. It generates privacy and cookie policies and terms and conditions documents, runs cookie consent banners and a consent database, and gives site owners a compliance dashboard. The homepage names plugins for content management systems such as WordPress, and the pricing page lists a REST API for custom integrations.
