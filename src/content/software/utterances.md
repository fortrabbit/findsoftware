utterances is a comments widget that keeps the comments of a page as issues in a GitHub repository. Readers post after they authorize the utterances app through GitHub's OAuth flow, or they comment on the issue directly on GitHub. The widget is written in TypeScript.

## Worth knowing

Comments depend on GitHub: the issues that hold them and the accounts that post.

The repository has no tagged release.
